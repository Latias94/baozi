# Closed Text Planning: Qualification and Unresolved Performance

The closed-text row and four ordinary inline hints remain an isolated development
candidate. The source passes its earlier full correctness qualification and lowers
whole-worker OBJ instructions, but the ordinary observations do not establish a
measured performance keep or main merge. Release remains **HOLD**: complete pipelines
still exceed the original 3x boundary, and several exceed 10x in both candidate rounds.

The [all-lane table](2026-11-06-closed-text-planning-lanes.md) preserves every original
stage, I/O mode, candidate maximum, and comparable process-memory ratio. Favorable OBJ
results do not replace the remaining glTF, STL, preparation, extraction, and PLY costs.
