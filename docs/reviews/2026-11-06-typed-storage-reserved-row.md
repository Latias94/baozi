## Ownership change

The typed factory admits its complete work before entering the builder. Its old
per-element path nevertheless used general grouped completion, repeatedly checking
frame identity, lifecycle and projected effective consumption. General callers can
legally tighten budgets or complete physical prefixes after a ledger fault; this fresh
typed factory has a more restricted interval.

The private `ReservedProducerRow` checks that interval once and borrows the actual
work entry and frame remainder. With consumed C, pending P, remaining R, attempted A
and limit L, entry requires representable `C + P <= min(A, L)` and `P >= R`. The
exclusive loan excludes competing reservations and policy changes. A normally returned
action immediately transfers `(C, P, R)` to `(C + 1, P - 1, R - 1)`, preserving that
invariant. There is no cached allowance, deferred completion or second ledger.

The actual positive-remainder gate stays before the callback. The existing 256-unit
typed polling cadence, first-error order, incomplete-work checks and destruction
containment remain. The outer host stays armed, resolves and drops after the short
control loan ends, and drops before the final conditional poll. Entry denial settles
the admitted host and releases prepared storage. General grouped completion, its
`CompletedWithLedgerFailure` prefix behavior and attempted-only parser checks remain.
